Priority rule:

- Priority 0 keeps the current working board/toolchain/SD/XMM baseline safe.
- Priority 1 covers the most basic useful work for a MicroPython-class Berry system: standard Berry compatibility, SD imports, `.be`/`.bec`, and PSRAM cache/staging.
- Priority 2 covers useful Propeller 2 hardware APIs, hardware tests, and examples that should not destabilize the VM.
- Priority 3 and later are hard/high-risk or lower immediate value: PASM bridges, real closure-per-cog execution, cooperative tasks, video/USB, advanced debug, and performance experiments.
